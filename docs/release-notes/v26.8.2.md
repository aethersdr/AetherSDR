### Before you upgrade

Four things change behaviour you may be relying on. Everything else is a straight upgrade.

- **If you run a non-Flex backend on v26.8.1, take this release.** A null panadapter stream crashed the GUI thread on connect, on **Hermes-Lite 2 and KiwiSDR** as well as Icom — any backend that decodes its own scope was killed by the first dBm range it reported. No user action was needed to trigger it; it fired by itself on connect. (#4786)
- **Hermes-Lite 2: RX audio latency rises by a constant 64.0 ms.** Filter length also sets the *narrowest possible notch*, and 2048 taps floors a notch at 200 Hz — wide enough to swallow a CW signal sitting beside the carrier you are notching. RX filter taps go 2048 → 8192, and the cost is paid on every HL2 receiver whether or not you ever place a notch. (#4780)
- **MNR removes far more noise than it did, and its `Enable MNR` checkbox is gone.** Maximum-strength stationary attenuation measured **4.54 dB**; it now measures **24.4 dB**. Your existing strength setting will sound very different — start lower than you are used to. The method selector is now the sole enable, consistent with every other AetherDSP method. (#4807)
- **US 60m follows the FCC Report & Order that took effect on 13 February 2026.** The new **5351.5 – 5366.5 kHz** segment is drawn with its own colour so the 9.15 W ERP boundary reads straight off the panadapter, and the retired 5358.5 kHz channel is removed. The four retained channels keep their historic numbering (1, 2, 4, 5), so markers still agree with your existing logs. (#4762)

Radio-family status is unchanged: **FlexRadio remains the supported target.** Hermes-Lite 2 stays **experimental** and networked Icom is **early** — see [Supported Hardware](https://github.com/aethersdr/AetherSDR/blob/v26.8.2/README.md#supported-hardware).

### Headlines

**A third radio family: networked Icom.**

- New **`IcomCIV` backend** for networked Icoms, brought up against a live IC-705 and then an IC-9700. Connect and authenticate in ~250 ms, panadapter at 30 sweeps/s, RX audio, TCI RX, and receive handedness certified against WWV (USB/DIGU within 0.5 dB, LSB/DIGL within 0.6 dB). FT8 both decodes and spots on PSK Reporter over two wireless hops. CI-V is Icom's documented command plane; the RS-BA1 UDP transport it travels inside is documented nowhere, and was implemented behaviourally with kappanhang (MIT) attributed and wfview read as specification only. (#4784)
- An Icom remembers its own frequency, mode and filter across power cycles, so this backend never pushes restored state — it *reads* the radio's own levels at connect. Only the **IC-705 and IC-7300MK2** are marked `verified` against their own CI-V guides; an unrecognised model falls back to no scope and no transmit rather than optimistic defaults. (#4784)
- **IC-705 bring-up.** RF Gain, preamp and attenuator now say what they mean — the gain slider had been driving the preamp. Manual notch is wired to the radio's own `MN` rather than Flex-only DSP buttons an Icom cannot do; filter buttons follow the mode and read narrow→wide; AF gain, TX monitor, VOX, RIT/XIT and the ATU are wired and adopted at connect. The connect dialog stops flipping the radio type as you type an IP, and the password is now saved **after** a successful connect — it used to be written before the attempt, so a failed connect destroyed the stored one. The **60–90 second CI-V stall** was a session token renewed by a single fire-and-forget datagram against a 60 s expiry: one lost packet silently expired the session while the transport stayed healthy. (#4799)
- **IC-9700 support**, and three more faults with it: a dBm auto-floor that ran away at 24 dB/s because it waited for an echo a radio with no display-range command plane can never send; configuration advice emitted on a fatal channel, so a healthy session tore itself down 4 ms after coming up and reconnected every 5 s forever; and a MOD Input warning that demanded `MOD Input = WLAN` on a LAN-only radio. (#4786)
- **AetherModem transmits on a seam backend.** The built-in AX.25 modem could not transmit at all on a backend that takes TX audio over the seam — it gated on `hostModulates`, took the DAX branch, and waited on a stream a networked Icom has never had. Nothing logged an error, because every layer was correct by its own lights. Verified on an IC-9700. (#4812)
- Bringing a third vendor up also flushed out shared defects invisible from a Flex: `MeterModel` ignored the unit it was given, so an honest 5 watts of `TX:FWDPWR` arrived as 0.003 W, and the NR/NB/ANF/squelch setters emitted Flex wire text with no backend verb behind them, so no other backend could implement them however much it wanted to. (#4784)

**Hermes-Lite 2 closes two more gaps against a Flex.**

- **Manual notch filters on the panadapter** — right-click a signal to add or remove a notch, drag to move, drag vertically to resize. WDSP's vendored snapshot already carried a 1024-entry notch database that was never declared in the port shim, so nothing above could reach it. Measured off-air against WWV on 10 MHz: **33.4 dB** on the carrier, tracking held across two 8 kHz NCO moves, mirror frequency left alone within 1.8 dB. (#4780)
- **Manual frequency calibration** — a Calibration page in Radio Setup and one per-radio ppb correction applied to every frequency the app sends. The HL2 tunes off a free-running 38.4 MHz crystal and no register in the HPSDR map accepts a correction, so the host applies one or nobody does. One multiplicative scalar covers every band, transmit included. Stored keyed by MAC, so an operator with two HL2s does not get one's calibration silently applied to the other. (#4783)
- **The first connect no longer freezes the app** for 21–82 seconds — reported as "the client has completely hung". FFTW was measuring `FFTW_PATIENT` plans correctly off the GUI thread, with the GUI thread standing there waiting for all of it. The UI now stays live throughout (219 of 219 automation pings answered, longest gap 0.50 s, against 0 of ~200 before). Separately the wisdom was **never saved** — export was an `std::atexit` handler, which SIGTERM, a crash and a Force Quit all skip, so anyone who had ever force-quit paid first-run cost on every run. Next launch is now 0.57 s. (#4775)
- **AM/SAM audio is DC-blocked.** WDSP's AM/SAM detector is an envelope detector, so the carrier lands in the audio as a DC pedestal — and measured on a 50%-modulated carrier that pedestal alone sat **79% past full scale** after AGC. AM audio was clipping against the rails everywhere downstream, not merely drawing an odd waveform. (#4774)
- **CWX, DVK and FDX are hidden on radios that cannot do them** rather than sitting permanently dim, which reads as a fault to go looking for instead of a fact about the radio. The F1–F12 keyer shortcuts are gated too. (#4777)

**SPE Expert amplifiers — 1.3K-FA / 1.5K-FA / 2K-FA.**

- A second peripheral amplifier following the ACOM precedent, entirely outside the radio seam. Serial (115200 8N1) or ser2net TCP, raw **or** telnet, both verified on real hardware; poll-silence detection, so a TCP link that outlives the amp being switched off is reported as such; model identification from the status ID field; and an RFC 2217 DTR/RTS **power-ON pulse** that works over the network. The applet carries power / antenna-SWR / ATU-SWR gauges — the power gauge rescales with the selected LOW/MID/HIGH level, matching the amp's own display — plus V/I/temperature readouts, warning and alarm banners, and the front-panel keystrokes. (#4531)

**Three new spot and schedule overlays.**

- **N1MM+ / DXLog contest bandmap spots** over a SmartSDR-CAT-compatible UDP XML listener, with dupe / needed-multiplier / CQ / busy / bust status on the panadapter and eight configurable status→colour swatches. Default port 12060 matches the `FocusHelperN1MMPort` SmartSDR documents, so existing broadcast configs work unchanged. N1MM is the only feed that explicitly says when to add, update and remove, so spots are keyed by callsign+band rather than deduped by frequency. (#4678)
- **The EiBi shortwave broadcast schedule** in its own SpotHub tab — auto-start, live spot counter, forced re-download, conditional `If-Modified-Since` updates on a 7-day threshold, and a customisable spot colour that repaints active markers live. Resolves 289 country codes, 560 language/mode codes, target areas and 1,629 transmitter sites into rich-text tooltips. (#4822)
- **The KiwiSDR DX Community database** as an independent, **opt-in** overlay for shortwave utility, beacon, broadcast and DX spots: cyan diamond markers on the band-plan strip, tooltips with frequency, station, mode, passband cuts and notes, and click-to-tune that sets frequency, filter bandwidth and — with SpotHub's **Auto** on — the mode, through a normalising whitelist so an unrecognised mode string can never reach the radio as a command. (#4828)
- Smaller items in the same area: the **SpotHub Time column sorts** at last (only Freq ever returned a sort value, so once you sorted by frequency there was no way back) (#4751); a **status-message field in the FreeDV Reporter panel** that stays in sync with SpotHub → FreeDV (#4757); and **KiwiSDR receiver lists import and export as CSV**, merging on a normalised endpoint so re-importing the same file is idempotent — passwords are never exported, they stay in the OS keychain (#4753).

**Spectrum, waterfall and 3D FFT.**

- **K4-style mini-pan** — a small, detachable ±5/±10 kHz window centred on the active VFO, independent of the main panadapter and usable when it is hidden (Minimal Mode) or alongside third-party contest logging. It is an independent top-level window, so it floats over other applications and survives AetherSDR being minimised. Live on a FLEX-6500 at ~26 Hz/bin, five times finer than the 200 kHz main pan. (#4562)
- **The 3D stacked-trace wedges close.** Every row covered the same frequency span while the far rows narrowed with perspective, leaving two empty black triangles flanking the surface. A new **3D Span** slider sits under Display → 3D VIEW, and **0 restores today's rendering exactly**. (#4779)
- **3D FFT works on the Raspberry Pi 5 again** — `dss_mesh` is the only shader using the `flat` qualifier, no 140 slice existed, and the Pi's v3d driver fell back to a 120 slice the shader cannot compile against. Desktop GPUs picked the 150 slice, which is why CI never saw it. (#4754) **3D FFT history outlines render on Linux/OpenGL** too. (#4730)
- **Windows panadapters are no longer blank under software OpenGL** — with `AETHER_NO_GPU` or `QT_OPENGL=software`, the panadapter chose OpenGL while the hidden WAVE scope had already chosen D3D11 for the same top-level window, and Qt rejects mixed QRhi APIs in one backing store (13,804 `QRhiWidget: No QRhi` messages, every panadapter transparent). The default D3D11 path is unchanged. (#4748)
- **A headless Wayland session automatically prefers xcb** — the typical VNC/wayvnc case, where there is no DRM scanout, so every frame's `makeCurrent` fails with `EGL_BAD_MATCH` and `AETHER_NO_GPU=1` does not help. Detection is deliberately tri-state, a user-set `QT_QPA_PLATFORM` still wins, and the decision is logged at startup so it is diagnosable from a support bundle. (#4755)
- **Waterfall interpolation is clamped at history boundaries**, ending the occasional bright, dark or scrambled horizontal line during rapid pan or zoom (#4794), and **Waterfall Blanker rows stay in one frequency frame** instead of permanently retaining a shifted row (#4802).
- **A GPU that fails mid-session now says so** — `renderFailed()` fired with nothing listening, leaving a blank panadapter and no explanation. Each GPU spectrum widget now shows a persistent warning card over the affected pan. (#4787)
- **Off-screen slice indicators centre on a single click** — the active indicator, the one you are most likely to be chasing, emitted nothing at all. (#4795)

**Receive DSP and audio.**

- **MNR delivers the attenuation it advertised** — its minimum-statistics estimator took a raw minimum across 25 periodograms and left the Wiener gain near unity; 4.54 dB becomes **24.4 dB**, scale-invariant within 1 dB, with +18.8 dB SNR improvement on speech-like input for −2.8 dB of desired-signal level. (#4807)
- **DFNR no longer pumps on stereo** — it drove a delayed dry stereo signal with a fast processed-to-dry power envelope, the same family of fault already fixed in RN2. (#4788)
- **The AetherVoice EQ canvas caches its layers** instead of rebuilding the band response, grid, labels and band-plan decoration on the GUI thread every analyzer frame: on the same Windows VM, 73.1% → 37.4% of one core at 1280×900, and 82.8% → 43.1% at 2200×1300. (#4808)
- **Audio mute commands reach the model.** The three mute setters sent their command and returned, never assigning and never emitting, and `FlexBackend` is the only parser of those statuses — so on every other backend the flags sat at their `false` initialisers for the whole session: mute the headphones, nudge the volume, and the glyph silently flipped back. (#4772) The title-bar headphone mute also **reconciles with the radio**, so a second Multi-Flex client changing it no longer leaves the icon lying (#4733), and the header speaker emoji follows local PC audio mute (#4628).

**CW keying, to spec.**

- **Iambic sidetone timing.** `workerLoop()` armed each deadline as `now() + duration`, read *after* the previous wait returned, so every element ran long by a fresh error that never self-corrected. Deadlines now advance on an absolute `steady_clock` grid anchored at squeeze start, tracked in nanoseconds, and key transitions are timestamped into a lock-free ring and mapped to exact sample offsets instead of being quantised to an audio-block boundary. **Measured on real hardware (Intel MBP, HaliKey Serial): 45.70 ms/unit → 40.002 ms/unit at a 30 WPM setting — 26.26 WPM → 29.998 WPM.** (#4823)
- **Mode B no longer drops the trailing element on a clean simultaneous release.** The memory latch ran only at condition-variable wakes, and a wake caused by a release always observes post-release state, so the keyer behaved like Mode A — routine on the serial path, where a 10 ms poll collapses both edges into one event. (#4810)

**Copy Assist (speech-to-text).**

- **A GPU that cannot be used falls back to CPU instead of taking the app down.** On a machine whose Vulkan stack enumerates GPUs but cannot create a logical device, Copy Assist crashed the process silently. It is the *second* GPU attempt that kills it, not the first — nothing in `try`/`catch` can intercept it, so the fix is to never make the second attempt. The selector now labels unusable devices, resolves to the first usable one (else CPU), and says in plain language when a fallback has happened; previously the dialog went on naming a GPU that was running nothing while the queue grew from 7.6 s to 22.5 s with no explanation. (#4767)
- **Enabling speaker labels no longer freezes the GUI** — rebuilding the engine on the GUI thread performed an unbounded worker-thread join, so ONNX work already in flight blocked Qt event processing and could starve radio servicing. (#4739)
- **Segment overlap for boundary-word recovery** — when an utterance is force-closed by the max-length cap the cut can fall mid-word, and each segment is decoded independently, so the straddling word is mangled or dropped. A small window of trailing audio now carries into the next segment, at zero inference cost and across all three backends. **Opt-in, off by default** — a "Boundary overlap" slider at 0 ms. (#4837, RFC #4821)

**Controllers, MIDI and TCI.**

- **Rotary action mapping for the Ulanzi Dial** — a *Tuning:* rotary action selector covering frequency, filter bandwidth, slice and master volume, panadapter zoom, RIT/XIT, AGC-T, RF gain, APF, CW speed and RF power, with the dial-press combo relabelled *Single tap:*. Filter-preset stepping now scales by the magnitude of the rotary step rather than discarding it, and an unrecognised action ID is validated before dispatch and short-circuits as a harmless no-op. (#4702, #4758, #4834)
- **Manual MIDI binding entry and per-row editing** — a **Manual…** button beside Learn types in a binding's channel, message type and note/CC number directly, and a ✎ button on each row reopens the same form pre-filled to correct what Learn captured wrong, without re-learning. The settings format is untouched, so a file written by this build loads in older ones. (#4790)
- **TCI: a use-after-free in the DAX RX path** is fixed — reproduced under ASan on the parent commit, triggered deterministically by a client switching from 48 kHz to native 24 kHz with sub-threshold samples still staged. (#4789)
- **TCI: malformed `volume:` and `modulation:` input is rejected, not reinterpreted.** An empty `volume:` argument parsed to `0.0` — and 0 dB is the *top* of the range, so the malformed command landed on the loudest value it can express. An unrecognised modulation name silently set USB while the notification echoed the client's own wrong name. (#4848)
- The `aether.cat` log category is relabelled **"TCI / CAT / rigctld"** — `TciServer.cpp` is 69.7% of its call sites, and operators chasing a TCI problem scanned the checkbox grid, saw no mention of TCI, and reasonably concluded there was none to turn on. (#4761)

**Platform and packaging.**

- **Windows: `vcomp140.dll` now ships app-local.** `AetherSDR.exe` genuinely imports the MSVC OpenMP runtime, and our packaging never shipped it — leaving one file as the single machine-wide dependency in the entire payload, so any other installer that repaired, downgraded or removed the VC++ redistributable took AetherSDR down, and reinstalling AetherSDR could not fix it. All three Windows artifacts were affected. A new **`check-deploy-dependencies.ps1` CI gate** walks every PE in the payload and fails when an MSVC runtime import is not app-local, checked *before* the System32 probe — build machines have the redistributable installed, which is exactly how this shipped unnoticed through every release since ASR landed. (#4782)
- **Linux: the Connect to Radio dialog reopens reliably under xcb.** Once connected the dialog auto-hides, and reopening it called `show()` successfully — `isVisible()` reported `true` — yet nothing appeared, not even in the window switcher, making it impossible to disconnect. `xprop` confirmed the window wedged in the ICCCM `Withdrawn` state under Mutter/XWayland. This matters because `QT_QPA_PLATFORM=xcb` is the practical workaround for the native-Wayland render stalls in #4725. (#4803)

**GUI and workflow.**

- **A thumbnail view for the Choose Background Image dialog**, with an Explorer-style small-icons/thumbnails toggle and a preview pane that grows with the dialog. Decoding runs off the GUI thread behind the same decompression-bomb guard used for callsign photos. (#4726)
- **The analog power gauge picks the right scale at connect** instead of coming up on 120 W and only correcting after a band change. (#4845)
- **The FFTW wisdom popup stopped strobing** — a deliberate infinite opacity animation past 90% caused the reported flashing and, supplying no elapsed time or status, left a slow process still looking frozen. It is now a low-key activity indicator with a monotonic timer and a "still working" explanation after 10 quiet seconds. (#4729)
- **The PGXL fan-mode dropdown fits its widest item** — its hand-rolled stylesheet set a 10px font on the combo but not on its popup, a separate top-level window that does not inherit it, so "Fan: Contest" was elided on a large enough default font. Root cause and fix worked out by the triage bot on the issue. (#4731, #4752)
- **Every model-lifetime callback is installed once.** Two self-connections were owned by the `RadioModel` rather than the replaceable backend, so every Flex → HL2 → Sim family switch accumulated another capability publication and TX-power push. (#4599, #4626)

**Tests, tooling and docs.**

- **`hl2_tx_loopback_test` runs green and its exclusion is retired.** It had been carried as "pre-existing, fails non-deterministically, exclude it" — the "pre-existing" call was right and everything else was a misdiagnosis: **it was never a transmit fault.** The assertion predated the receive-side conjugation added in #4471, and the "non-determinism" was a hardcoded `192.168.1.12` that on this LAN belongs to a different machine, so the test was silently driving *that* box's simulator. It now takes an independent bearing using hpsdrsim's own scene tones, and **refuses to key anything that is not the simulator**. (#4800)
- An undefined downcast that **aborted the sanitizer job before a single result line printed** is gone. (#4740, #4741)
- `HERMES.md` gains §22 on WDSP channel-open cost and FFTW wisdom plus a corrected §14.6; `docs/CERTIFICATION.md` gains lessons 1.19–1.31 from the Icom bring-up — the first radio brought up with `radiocert` in hand rather than after the fact; and there are new design notes for the SPE amplifier, the Icom CI-V backend and HL2 frequency calibration.
